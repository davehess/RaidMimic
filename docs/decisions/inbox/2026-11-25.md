# Decisions inbox — 2026-11-25

### 656. [mimic-overlays] Blind opens the Map zoomed on the nearest walls; dashed lines from raiders to their targets; radar sense to the Desk (2026-11-25)
**The ask** (the guild lead): *"build the map into blind mode zoomed in enough to see where the closest walls are near or
directly above/below the player, the currently targeted mob's information should be visible at the top of the HUD so we don't
need to worry about that, but extended targets should also be displayed. while we're at it let's have dashed lines from other
targetters running mimic to their target. faint green for friendly/NPC, red for engaged mobs, yellow for mobs not
engaged/moving."* Then: *"I'd like to see a 'radar sense' mode when blind but that also requires us to know if a character was
zoomed in or out and how far, so that's tough."*
- **Built (beta, agent 4.0.110; bot 4.1.13 on main):**
  - Blind is now one of the overlays forced open, the Map included, in Window or Full mode.
  - While blind the Map follows you and zooms so the nearest wall on your floor is in view. The radius is 3 times that
    distance, kept between 60 and 250 units. Your floor (±25) is bright and the floors above and below are faint.
  - The ⚔ Engaged list (the extended targets) slides out.
  - Everything goes back when blind ends, and nothing is saved. Your mouse wheel stops the auto zoom.
- **The target's own information** needed nothing new: the HUD ring already carries the current target on its top arc.
- **Targeter lines:** the bot's map-sync now says who is on each NPC (`by`), within the same scope as the count. The Map draws
  a dashed line from each raider running Mimic to their target, with a dot at the mob.
  - **Red** means hurt: HP 1-99%, the Map's own "engaged" rule.
  - **Faint green** means your last /con of it was better than threatening.
  - **Yellow** means anything else: up and not engaged, standing or walking.
  - Lines go to NPC targets only. A player target never reaches the bot: the agent sends no zone or position for one.
- **Radar sense does not need EverQuest's camera.** The game screen is black while you are blind, so Mimic can use its own chase
  camera from your position and facing, which Zeal sends. The real question is which walls to draw, and that is a Decision Desk
  card (`radar-sense`, this month) with rendered pictures:
  - **A:** map lines raised into walls; recommended first.
  - **B:** the real EQEmu collision mesh.
  - **C:** no radar.
  - **Follow-ups:** where it shows, the camera, lines to player targets, and how to mark floors above and below.
- Tests: `test/map-blind.test.js`, `test/map-target-lines.test.js`, `test/raid-targets.test.js`.

### 657. [mimic-overlays] Radar sense built (Desk pick A), target size and reach, a test blind button with the split view (2026-11-25)
**The call** (the guild lead): *"let's try to implement that then, and show where the mob is with the target info, how large it
is and its attack range. I will need a button to test blind mode on beta for this without being blinded, maybe with split screen
to try to see what it looks like between the modes for testing"*. "That" was the recommendation on the Desk card `radar-sense`.
- **Built (beta, agent 4.0.111; bot 4.1.14 on main):**
  - Radar A in the Map window, drawn in Canvas 2D: no three.js in Mimic, since raised map lines are only lines.
  - The camera sits 42 behind you and 20 up, turned by Zeal's heading.
  - Walls are your map lines raised 20 tall: your floor bright, floors up to ±60 faint, within 160 of you.
  - People, mobs and the targeter lines draw through walls.
- **Size and attack range** come from the server's own rule: the bounding radius (race and size) and the combat range
  0.75 × (mob radius + 5), at least 14, +2 standing, capped 75 (60 rooted). Area Rampage already used this rule (`utils/aeRampage.js`
  `reachInfo`), and every NPC with a size gets it now. The radar and the 2D Map draw the body and a dashed ring at that reach,
  and the radar adds name, health, distance and "size N · hits at N".
- **Testing:** ⚙ → Blind view picks Map + radar (default, side by side), Radar, or Map. ⚙ → Test blind (30 s) sets a blind of
  source `test` through `POST /api/blind-test`, so every surface a real blind changes changes. It never overrides or ends a real
  blind, and no callout is spoken.
- **Limits written down:** wall heights are a guess, there are no ceilings, stairs or ramps (option B, the real mesh, would add
  them), and a zone with no map file has no walls. Still open on the card: lines to player targets, and marking floors above
  vs below.

### 658. [mimic-overlays] UI too small at 4K: no resolution writer; UI Studio resizes by any edge or corner (2026-11-25)
**The ask:** a member in Discord asked how to stop "tiny everything" at 3840×2160. The guild lead: *"the resolutions are built
into the EQ client for the client itself but it would be cool to resize UI in UI studio by drag/pull"*.
- **Mimic does not list or write video modes.** The client's resolution list is built in, so there is no monitor-modes picker
  and no `[VideoMode]` writer.
- **The answer for 4K:**
  - play at 1920×1080, which is exactly half, so the screen doubles every pixel;
  - or use Zeal's big-fonts mode (an empty `zeal_ui_skin.ini` in the skin folder, then a restart) with a 2× upscaled skin.
    None exists for Quarm that we know of, and Zeal ships none.
  - The client draws skin windows at fixed pixel sizes, so no ini edit makes them bigger.
- **Built (Mimic beta 2112ada9):** UI Studio resizes a window by any of its eight edges and corners, shown on hover.
  - Before this there was one bottom-right grip, shown only on the selected window.
  - It is pure `UiFit.resizeRect`, and Save writes the size.
- **Not built:** a true-size preview at another resolution with chat filled from the log since the last "Welcome to
  EverQuest!". It needs a new log-tail IPC and a confirmed chat filter map. It stays an idea until asked for again.
