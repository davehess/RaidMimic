### 604. [web] Light mode is parchment, never paper white; looks are tokens only (2026-11-17)

**The call.** The guild lead, on the first light look of the raidmimic.com redesign mocks: *"can we not do bright white
for the light mode? make it parchment - I was damn near blinded looking at that in low light."* And the day before, on
why looks exist at all: *"ideally I could have themes on this site because it might get annoying to look at the
engraved style all the time."*

- **Light mode is parchment on every surface we build** (raidmimic.com, wolfpack.quest, Mimic's local pages if they
  ever get one): no white or near-white page or panel. The mock values: page `#d6c7a6`, panel `#dfd1b2`, row `#cdbd9a`,
  line `#b8a582`, brown ink `#2b2116`. That puts the page at 58% luminance and panels at 65%; the rejected first try was
  88% / 97% (`#f5f1e8` / `#fffcf5`). Every text and status colour stays at or above 4.5:1 on page, panel and row (green
  `#145230`, orange `#723705`, red `#82241e`, blue `#1f4178`, gold text `#5e3e06`; the gold button carries dark ink).
- **Looks change tokens only, never layout:** a visitor picks Titles (Engraved or Plain, which swaps `--display`) and
  Mode (Dark, Light, System). One page works in every look, so a look costs one block of values. Guild themes keep
  their own slot: the guild sets accent and logo, the visitor sets titles and mode.
- **Picked later the same day** (the guild lead: *"default to dark mode, include all four versions so we can toggle
  between them. accents can be per person or per guild."*): **Dark is the default**; all four looks (Engraved or Plain
  titles × Dark or Parchment) are offered as four tiles in the Aa menu, remembered per device and per account. The
  titles default was not stated; Engraved is assumed (the recommendation) until the guild lead says otherwise.
- **Accents, per person or per guild, from a short list** checked in both modes: Gold (Raid Mimic's own), Silver,
  Violet, Teal, Rose. Never red, green or orange (bad, good, warning) and never blue: a Frost accent was rendered and the
  plane marked "now on" (blue) stopped standing out among the cleared ones. On parchment a light accent fill separates
  from the page at only 1.5 to 1.7:1, so bars and the current-tab line use the dark accent there and filled buttons carry
  an ink outline.
- Open on the Desk: which wins when a person and a guild both set an accent (recommended: the person's, then the guild's
  on its own pages, then gold), and narrowing today's guild theme (`web/lib/guildThemeCore.ts` lets a guild set any
  background and text colour, which would paint over parchment; recommended: guilds keep accent, logo and Discord colour
  only), and whether wolfpack.quest gets the same menu.

**Where it landed.** Mocks on branch `draft/rm-redesign-2026-11-16` (`v2.css`, `guild2.html`, the `look-*.png` and
`accent-*.png` pictures; the Aa menu works by clicking there). The Desk card `rm-redesign`: pictures L0 to L4 and A1 to
A3, follow-ups `rm-redesign-looks` (answered), `rm-redesign-accent-order`, `rm-redesign-guild-theme`,
`rm-redesign-looks-wq`. Nothing on beta or main yet.

because the wildcard already points at Vercel). No per-guild Porkbun record, no Vercel Pro wildcard, no nameserver move, so
mail forwarding and DNSSEC are untouched. Hobby caps a project at 50 domains (16 used today); past about 30 guilds, revisit
(Pro, or moving DNS to Vercel with DNSSEC switched off first). Supersedes the "Pro plan, nameservers at Vercel" line of §490
and the self-host wizard §3 (both now point here).

**Access.** The Porkbun connector (hosted MCP at `mcp.porkbun.com`, its own "(connected app)" key, scoped by the guild lead to
"Manage, but no purchases"). A first attempt stored the two Porkbun header keys as network secrets, but the secure form keeps
one header per host, so only `X-Secret-API-Key` remained; that half-secret does nothing and can be deleted. Note for anyone
testing from a cloud session: the egress proxy re-signs TLS, so certificates seen from inside the container are not the real
ones; check with the Vercel API or a certificate-transparency log instead.

### 615. [bot-discord] Shared Raid Mimic bot: restart without a blip (2026-11-17, OPEN)

**The ask.** The guild lead: *"do it as a single shared railway bot and spin up a new one when we need to cut over for
upgrades? how would that work to get away from the downtime blips"*.

**What is true today.** Railway already starts a new copy per deploy; the Wolf Pack bot's lease (§562 stage 1) makes the
new copy wait until the old one logs out. Measured from the deploy log, the last 10 handovers (2026-11-16..17): SIGTERM
to `[lease] acquired` 2.2 to 13.3 s, about 7 s typical, plus the Discord login. Clicks in the gap fail; agent uploads
get 503 and retry from their queue. One shared bot puts that gap on every guild at once.

**Options on the Desk (card `bot-cutover`, when: week).** A (recommended): build the Raid Mimic bot on Discord's HTTP
interactions endpoint plus REST posting, with no gateway login; Railway's normal overlap then has no gap, two replicas can
run, and scheduled posts take a database claim row so only one replica posts. B: copy the Wolf Pack gateway bot and its
lease (about 7 s per deploy). C: two logged-in copies (§562 stage 2; needs `utils/liveMemory.js` retired first).
Follow-ups: replica count; shortening the Wolf Pack gap (1 s standby poll, release before the 6 s drain: check that
in-flight handlers do not need Discord after release); batching Wolf Pack bot deploys (10 in two days).
Unverified: whether discord.js can hand a gateway session to another process to RESUME (that would close B's gap too).

**§615 addendum: what a restart loses (read from `index.js` 2026-11-17).** The guild lead: *"The issue is what is stored
in memory, the mobs, the fights, etc. Does that hand over?"* The RECORDS do, through the database: fights, parses, mobs
and threat are built on each raider's agent, and every upload is written on arrival (encounters, contributions,
buff_casts; raid_roster and character_live_state batched every 2 s and flushed at SIGTERM; fight snapshots go to rows
AND files, `snapshot_store` = 1 in `overlay_tuning`). Agents queue on disk and retry the 503s of the gap. A new process
reads Supabase until warm (`utils/liveMemory.js`). Memory-only and LOST on restart: the open parse card (`_liveCards`,
whose fallback `getAgentTestCard` reads state.json, which Railway does not keep, so a later upload for the same boss
posts a second card instead of merging; from the code, not yet seen in a log), `_killAnnounced` (10 min) and
`_triggerDedup` (10 s) duplicate checks, `_lastCasterByTargetSpell` (3 h "who cast it"), `_debuffClearMarks` (officer
cured marks), and short-lived state that refills in seconds to minutes (`_castingByTarget`, `_reporterRegistry`,
`_extMobLastSeen`, `_extHurtSince`, `_buffCoverage`). A fight that spans a restart is written as two files, by design.
