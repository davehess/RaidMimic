# Decisions inbox — 2026-11-22

### 650. [infra-costs] Pro sells keeping: Free gets all of tonight's raid and each night's totals; detail and replays are kept only on Pro (2026-11-22)
**The call** (the guild lead, 2026-11-22, on the raidmimic.com pricing cards, in two messages): *"update the pricing so that
we can feasibly do parse or Target [syncing] for the current day and not retain if someone's not paying"*, then, correcting
the first cut: *"the whole point was that retention was the thing that cost us money ... retention was the thing that
needed to be paid and the rest of those items should be available via the bot that we're running for current day only for
those that aren't paying for it ... being able to have the parse totals at the end of the day and just be able to provide
that detail is something that we can retain but the actual minute by minute parse details and the raid fight replay that's
all stuff that would be paid for."*

Final shape (after the fourth message, below), as the pricing page's "What each plan keeps" table shows it:

| | Free | Pro $3 (list $5) | Pro $6 (list $10) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tonight's raid, all of it (merged parses, raid review, replay and spectator, Command Center, the bot) | yes | yes | yes |
| Second-by-second detail (events, rollups, threat) and the fight replay (positions) | 7 days | 7 days | 60 days |
| Parse totals, fight by fight (encounters, encounter_players) | 60 days | 60 days | 60 days |
| Raid reviews, chat and /who history | tonight | 60 days | 60 days |

**Fourth message** (the guild lead, same day: *"let's go with 60 days, and keep the last weeks worth of full logs while we
can to show the 'trial' of what it would look like with the full data set, and show the difference"*): Free's totals are
kept 60 days (not without limit); the last 7 days are kept in full on Free and the smaller size as a trial of the larger
size, labelled "offered while we can" so it can be withdrawn; the table under the price cards shows the difference.
**Pushback recorded:** with totals and a 7-day trial free, the $3 size adds only reviews, chat and /who history past
tonight. That is thin, and the next pricing pass should give it something or fold it into one size.

**No size keeps raids forever** (the guild lead, the same day, a third message: *"we also need to remove the promise to
indefinitely store these raids the main differentiators are how much data we're storing ... second to second updates ...
that's a larger cost than someone that just wants retention of the last 60 days without the verbosity"*). The two sizes
differ by verbosity, not by window: both keep 60 days (was 90 and "every raid kept", §640).

This widens §526 (Free syncing was flags and kills only, never the fight feed) and refines §552: a price is still never
explained as storage, but what Pro sells is now plainly keeping.

**"The current day" means the raid night, not the calendar day.** A raid runs 8:30 PM to 12:30 AM Eastern, past midnight,
so a calendar-day cut would split a night in two. Default: windows end at **6 AM in the guild's time zone** (tonight's
raid = until the next 6 AM; 7 days = the seventh such morning). A session's default, changeable.

**Cost check (pushback recorded):** keeping is the cost that only grows, so the split is right for storage. Reads are the
other metered cost (egress): a free guild opening tonight's replay and review reads the database during the night, and
that is not free to us. It stays small only while those reads are cached; watch it when other guilds go live.

**Where it landed:** copy only, on beta (web 2.2.23, reworked through 2.2.28; the terms, refunds and register pages follow
through `GUILD_PRICE_SENTENCE`; `TERMS_VERSION` 2026-11-22): the Free and Pro cards, the pricing lead ("Free gives you all
of tonight's raid. Pro keeps it."), the "What each plan keeps" table (`KEEP_ROWS`, `FULL_TRIAL_DAYS` in
`web/lib/raidmimicStory.ts`) and the onboarding Free/Pro table. Nothing sweeps a free guild's data yet: the commons
(`utils/openHub.js`, `docs/DESIGN-open-hub.md`) is dormant, and its `commons` `RETENTION` windows must become "detail and
positions 7 days, totals 60 days, reviews/chat/who until the next 6 AM" when it is built.
`docs/DESIGN-selfhost-wizard.md` §3 carries the line.
Open: the open-hub design's 12-player cap on a free guild is not on the pricing page.

### 651. [infra-costs] No refunds: Pro is a subscription (2026-11-22)
**The call** (the guild lead, 2026-11-22: *"I don't want to offer refunds it's a subscription service"*, then *"how about no
full refunds and we take out that language"*, then pick **A**, plain "no refunds", over "no full refunds" with an undefined
partial refund): the 7-day refund for an unused month (the pre-launch checklist, 2026-11-01) is gone. The pages say "There
are no refunds." Cancelling stops the next charge and Pro runs to the end of the paid month; that month is not refunded.

**Removed at the guild lead's call:** the sentence that the policy does not take away a refund the law where you live
gives you. The session had recommended keeping it (some places give a withdrawal right on online subscriptions that a
merchant's policy cannot remove, and the page now does not mention it); the right exists either way. **Kept:** the line
for billing mistakes (charged twice, or after cancelling), which are fixed rather than refused. Not legal advice.

**Where it landed:** beta, web 2.2.26 (settled in 2.2.28): the refunds page (same `/refunds` address: the footer, the
sitemap and the terms link it), the pricing card's link reads "no refunds", the terms' line, the page's link-preview
description, and `TERMS_VERSION` 2026-11-22 (also covers §650's changes). The terms' "going back to Free does not delete a
guild's records" became "keeps parse totals for 60 days and the last 7 days in full; anything older goes", which §650 made
necessary.
