# Decisions inbox — 2026-11-22

### 650. [infra-costs] Free shares tonight's raid live and keeps none of it; keeping it is Pro (2026-11-22)
**The call** (the guild lead, 2026-11-22, on the raidmimic.com pricing cards: *"update the pricing so that we can feasibly do
parse or Target [syncing] for the current day and not retain if someone's not paying"*): the Free plan includes the live,
raid-wide sharing for the night being played (the merged parse and shared target info, the cross-client Mob Info), and
none of it is kept. Keeping raids (the last 90 days, or every raid) is what Pro · Guild pays for. This widens §526, which
had Free syncing send flags and kills only, never the fight feed.

**Why it is feasible:** what costs us is storage that only grows and reads that leave Supabase (`CLAUDE.md`, Supabase). A
night's fight rows deleted by morning never accumulate, and the live reads during the night are served from the bot's
memory where they already are (`utils/liveMemory.js`).

**"The current day" means the raid night, not the calendar day.** A raid runs 8:30 PM to 12:30 AM Eastern, past midnight,
so a calendar-day cut would split a night in two. Default: a free guild's night is swept at **6 AM in the guild's time
zone**. A session's default, changeable.

**Where it landed:** copy only, on beta (web 2.2.23): the Free card, the pricing lead ("Free shares tonight's raid. Pro
keeps it and reads it back to you.") and the onboarding Free/Pro table. Nothing sweeps a free guild's night yet: the
commons (`utils/openHub.js`, `docs/DESIGN-open-hub.md`) is dormant, and its `RETENTION` windows (2 to 60 days on the
`commons` plan) must drop to "until 6 AM guild time" when it is built. `docs/DESIGN-selfhost-wizard.md` §3 carries the line.
Open: whether a free guild's players keep their own copy in Mimic (the open-hub design says history lives on the PC).
