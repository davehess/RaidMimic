# Decisions inbox — 2026-11-22

### 650. [infra-costs] Pro sells keeping: Free gets all of tonight's raid and each night's totals; detail and replays are kept only on Pro (2026-11-22)
**The call** (the guild lead, 2026-11-22, on the raidmimic.com pricing cards, in two messages): *"update the pricing so that
we can feasibly do parse or Target [syncing] for the current day and not retain if someone's not paying"*, then, correcting
the first cut: *"the whole point was that retention was the thing that cost us money ... retention was the thing that
needed to be paid and the rest of those items should be available via the bot that we're running for current day only for
those that aren't paying for it ... being able to have the parse totals at the end of the day and just be able to provide
that detail is something that we can retain but the actual minute by minute parse details and the raid fight replay that's
all stuff that would be paid for."*

| | Free | Pro · Guild |
|---|---|---|
| Tonight's raid: merged parses, raid review, replay and spectator, Command Center, the Discord bot | yes, for the night | yes |
| Each night's parse totals (per fight, per raider) | kept | kept |
| Minute-by-minute parse detail (events, rollups, threat) and the fight replay (positions) | gone by morning | kept: 90 days ($3 early bird) or every raid ($6) |

This widens §526 (Free syncing was flags and kills only, never the fight feed) and refines §552: a price is still never
explained as storage, but what Pro sells is now plainly keeping.

**"The current day" means the raid night, not the calendar day.** A raid runs 8:30 PM to 12:30 AM Eastern, past midnight,
so a calendar-day cut would split a night in two. Default: a free guild's detail is swept at **6 AM in the guild's time
zone**. A session's default, changeable.

**Cost check (pushback recorded):** keeping is the cost that only grows, so the split is right for storage. Reads are the
other metered cost (egress): a free guild opening tonight's replay and review reads the database during the night, and
that is not free to us. It stays small only while those reads are cached; watch it when other guilds go live.

**Where it landed:** copy only, on beta (web 2.2.23, reworked in 2.2.24): the Free and Pro cards, the pricing lead ("Free
gives you all of tonight's raid. Pro keeps it.") and the onboarding Free/Pro table. Nothing sweeps a free guild's detail
yet: the commons (`utils/openHub.js`, `docs/DESIGN-open-hub.md`) is dormant, and its `commons` `RETENTION` windows must
become "detail until 6 AM guild time; encounters and encounter_players (the totals) kept" when it is built.
`docs/DESIGN-selfhost-wizard.md` §3 carries the line.
