# Decisions inbox — 2026-11-16

### 598. [pop-progression] /pop/raid is the tier page; "not recorded" is never "missing" (2026-11-16)

- **The ask (the guild lead):** *"https://b.wolfpack.quest/pop/raid needs to be split into tiers, starting from the highest
  tier and moving down. Least wordy as possible, give high level 'who's missing what' per section. Collapse everything and
  have them be expandable or click into it on a sidebar"*. Two layouts went to beta; **B (folded tiers) was picked on the
  Decision Desk** over C (tier sidebar), with both follow-ups: keep Tier One as one line, and bring the 85/15 line back inside
  the Elemental and Time tiers.
- **Why "not recorded":** the flag records called 19 of the 40 raiders seen in Plane of Air on 2026-11-15 unflagged, which
  cannot be true (the 85% door allows 6 for 40). `pop_flags` only knows what a full read, a hail or Mimic saw. So a raider is
  "missing" a piece only with a full read (Seer recital or #popflags) in the last 14 days; otherwise "not recorded". The 85%
  door line says passes or fails only when that holds whichever way the not recorded turn out, else how many it hangs on.
  The 85/15 "who benefits" list made from those records was withdrawn as wrong.
- **Where it landed:** web 2.2.10 on `main`, the same files on `beta`; `web/lib/popRaidTiers.ts`, `popRaidEntry.ts`
  (`doorLine`), `web/app/pop/raid/{TierPage,TierBlocks,PlanSectionView}.tsx`, `loadFullReads` in `web/lib/raidPreflags.ts`.

### 600. [mimic-app] PoP flags on this PC, with no account and no network (2026-11-16)

### 601. [infra-costs] Discord's sign-in screen shows raidmimic.supabase.co: a free vanity subdomain, not the $10 domain (2026-11-16)

- **The ask (the guild lead):** *"can we update that to say raidmimic.com"*, about Discord's consent screen, which named the
  project's own address. Options: a custom domain `auth.raidmimic.com` ($10 a month, not under the spend cap) or a free
  vanity subdomain `raidmimic.supabase.co`. **The guild lead took the vanity subdomain** and activated it with the Supabase CLI
  after adding `https://raidmimic.supabase.co/auth/v1/callback` to the Discord app's redirects.
- **Consequences:** the old `<project-ref>.supabase.co` address keeps working and the site keeps using it (switching the
  client URL would rename the sign-in cookies and sign everyone out). wolfpack.quest sign-in shows the same address, since
  both sites share one project and one Discord app. Moving to the custom domain later means removing the vanity subdomain
  first and adding one more Discord redirect. Recorded in `docs/DESIGN-selfhost-wizard.md` §3.
